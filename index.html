Programme rationale

Why We Are Asking

Scope 3 emissions, the emissions embedded in the products and services we buy, account for 71% of The Corporate's total carbon footprint on a location based basis, measured against our 2023 base year. That is a larger share than everything we control directly. We cannot reach our targets by looking only at our own operations.

We need your cooperation to reduce our Scope 3 emissions. This assessment is how we build a shared, factual picture of that footprint across our Tier 1 supply base, so we can set realistic goals together and support the suppliers who need it most.

Suppliers who demonstrate strong sustainability performance also receive preferential standing in our procurement scoring. Regulatory frameworks, including the EU's Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive, now require this kind of value chain reporting. That context matters, but it is not why we are asking. We are asking because reducing our shared footprint depends on working with you, not around you.
